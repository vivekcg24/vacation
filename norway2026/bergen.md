# Bergen, Norway
* **Time Windows:** 12:00 PM – 08:00 PM

* **Fløibanen Funicular Experience**
  Walk 5 minutes from the market to the lower station (fully step-free with elevator access). Board the smooth, modern rail car for the 6-minute ride to the summit. Spend 45 minutes resting on the flat viewing terraces overlooking the fjord, then ride back down. Ticket [link](https://www.floyen.no/en/floibanen/buy-tickets)
* **Bergens-Expressen Mini-Train Tour**
  Walk back to the harbor and board the mini-train for a 50-minute tour. It gives the older women a completely seated sightseeing route through the city's outer historic highlights and up another beautiful panoramic hillside without any physical walking required.
* **Scenic Byparken Rest & Cafés**
  Take a flat, 10-minute stroll over to Byparken, a beautiful public park surrounding a central lake. The paths are smooth pavement, lined with plenty of benches, blooming flower gardens, and nearby accessible cafes for coffee and traditional Norwegian pastries.
* **Souvenir Shopping & Harbor Walk**
  Head slowly back towards the ship along the flat harbor front, stopping at the accessible boardwalk shops to pick up local gifts or woolens.
