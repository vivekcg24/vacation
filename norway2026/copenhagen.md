# Copenhagen, Denmark
* **Time Windows:** 09:00 AM – 05:00 PM

* **Classic Nyhavn Canal Views**
  Arrive at Nyhavn, the iconic waterfront district lined with colorful 17th-century townhouses. Walk slowly along the wide, flat boardwalk. There are plenty of benches to sit, relax, and watch the wooden ships in the canal.
* **Covered Grand Canal Boat Tour**
  Board a classic canal boat tour directly from the flat Nyhavn docks. Opt for the glass-roofed, climate-controlled boats. The 1-hour cruise is completely seated and glides smoothly past the Little Mermaid statue, Amalienborg Palace, and the Opera House with zero walking required.raditional Danish *Smørrebrød* (open-faced sandwich) at a relaxed, slow pace.
* **Amalienborg Palace Square Stroll**
  Take a flat, 10-minute stroll from Nyhavn down the smooth waterfront path to Amalienborg Palace, the winter residence of the Danish Royal Family. The massive open cobblestone courtyard is level and easy to navigate to see the Royal Guards.
* **Taxi / Shuttle Ride to the Little Mermaid & Rest**
  Take a quick 5-minute taxi to the famous **Little Mermaid** statue. View it easily from the wide, paved upper promenade paths without descending the steps to the rocky shore.
* **Return Transit to Ship**
  Catch a taxi or the cruise shuttle directly back to the Oceankaj terminal.
