# 🗺️ Road Trip Itinerary: Heidelberg to Brüel (With Timberjacks Göttingen)

The optimal driving route from **Heidelberg** to **Pension Sonnenblick in Brüel** covers approximately **710 km and takes roughly 7 hours and 15 minutes** via the **A7 motorway**. 

## 📈 Finalized Route Overview & Key Stops

--- 

## 🛑 Stop 1: 

Knüllwald (After ~2 Hours of Driving) Located right on the A7 artery in central Hesse, this is the perfect milestone for your first quick leg stretch and morning coffee. * **Raststätte Hasselberg Ost**: A modern, highly accessible Serways station located directly on the northbound lanes. It features clean restroom facilities, an Aral fuel station, and an integrated Brotzeit bakery shop for quick pastries and espresso.

## 🍔 Stop 2: 
Göttingen Area (The Main Lunch Stop — After ~2.5 Hours) Instead of eating fast food, take a 2-minute detour off **Exit 73 (Göttingen-Dransfeld)** to experience **Timberjacks Göttingen** (*An den Weiden 3, 37081 Göttingen*). This enormous, authentic wooden log-cabin restaurant is tailor-made for road trippers.

* **🅿️ Parking Advantage:** Features a gargantuan, completely free private open-air parking lot surrounding the property. The spaces are oversized, making it entirely stress-free to park large vehicles, SUVs, or cars loaded with luggage. 
 
* For the Spice Lover: Sizzling Mexican Fajitas crusted in cayenne pepper, heavily spiced chili-con-carne, and fire-grilled chicken wings tossed in blistering hot habanero or jalapeño barbecue rubs. 

* For the European Palate: Premium wood-fired ribeye steaks, classic cheeseburgers, standard baked potatoes with sour cream, and mild Caesar salads. * **🕒 Operating Hours:** Open daily from 11:30 AM until midnight (kitchen closes late). 

## 🛑 Stop 3: 

Essel / Allertal (After ~4.5 Hours Total Driving) Before you exit the major A7 motorway to cross over the country toward Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, make one final quick pitstop. * **Serways Raststätte Allertal Ost: Conveniently situated directly alongside the highway. It is well-regarded by travelers for having excellent premium coffee to beat mid-afternoon fatigue, alongside an outdoor area if you need a quick breath of fresh air.

## 🏨 Your Destination: 

Brüel Your trip concludes at Pension Sonnenblick (*Wariner Str. 70, 19412 Brüel*). This highly rated lakeside property features its own private beach access, complimentary Wi-Fi, and free on-site parking.
