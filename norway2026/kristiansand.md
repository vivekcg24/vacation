# Kristiansand, Norway
* **Time Windows:** 01:00 PM – 09:00 PM


* **City Train Tour (Kilden Teater-og Konserthus)**
  Board the local tourist road train near the fish market. This gives the older women a fully seated, 50-minute tour of the city's historic quarters and seaside parks.
* **Posebyen Old Town Stroll**
  Explore the historic "Posebyen" district. It features the largest collection of low, white wooden houses in Northern Europe. The streets follow a flat, easy grid system.
* **Markens Gate Coffee & Shopping**
  Head to the main pedestrian street. It is completely level, paved, and filled with local boutiques, bakeries, and accessible benches for resting.
* **Walk Back to Pier**
  Enjoy a slow, flat walk back along the waterfront path toward the ship.
