# Oslo, Norway
* **Time Windows:** 08:00 AM – 04:00 PM

* **Rådhusbrygga (City Hall Pier) & Nobel Peace Center**
  Take a flat, 10-minute stroll along the wide waterfront promenade to the City Hall area. Enjoy the harbor views and view the beautiful, accessible courtyard of the Oslo City Hall.
* **Tram Ride to the Oslo Opera House**
  Board the smooth, low-floor Line 12 tram right at the harbor. Ride it through the flat city center to the iconic Oslo Opera House. The older women can sit inside the main lobby cafe, which features massive glass windows looking out over the Oslofjord.
* **Aker Brygge Lunch & Waterfront Stroll**
  Take the tram back to Aker Brygge, a vibrant pedestrian boardwalk. It is completely level, paved, and lined with indoor and outdoor restaurants. Sit by the water for a relaxed lunch of local Norwegian open-faced sandwiches.
* **Low-Floor Hop-On Hop-Off Bus Tour**
  Board the city sightseeing bus right at Aker Brygge. The lower deck is low-to-the-ground and fully accessible. This provides a completely seated, 45-minute loop to see outer highlights like the Royal Palace and Vigeland Sculpture Park without any uphill walking.
* **Flat Walk Back via the Harbor Promenade**
  Enjoy a slow, easy stroll along the wide waterfront path back toward the cruise ship.
