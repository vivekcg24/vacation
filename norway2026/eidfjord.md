# Eidfjord, Norway
* **Time Windows:** 08:00 AM – 06:00 PM

## ⏱️ Timeline & Route

### Eidfjord 3-Hour Rental Car Itinerary (11:00 AM – 2:00 PM)

* **11:00 AM – 11:15 AM: Vehicle Pickup**
  Walk 1 minute from the tender pier to Eidfjord Autorent. Collect pre-arranged keys, GPS, and toll pass.
* **11:15 AM – 11:40 AM: Scenic Drive & Photo Pull-offs**
  Drive east on Route Rv7. Pause quickly at **Eidfjordvatnet Lake** and the **Måbødalen Valley Pull-off** for rapid photos before navigating the spiral tunnels.
* **11:40 AM – 12:30 PM: Vøringsfossen Waterfall & Fossli Hotel**
  Park at the main lot. Walk the stepbridge over the 182-meter canyon. Stop inside the **Fossli Hotel Cafeteria** to grab a quick coffee or Norwegian waffles to go.
* **12:30 PM – 12:55 PM: Return Drive to Port**
  Drive west back down Route Rv7 directly to the pier terminal using engine braking ("B" mode) to control speed.
* **12:55 PM – 1:10 PM: Rapid Car Drop-off**
  Park the vehicle at the pier lot. Return the keys to the counter. Tolls are automatically tracked via the AutoPASS tag.
* **1:10 PM – 2:00 PM: Safety Buffer & Ship Boarding**
  Clear cruise security lines. Board the tender boat back to the MSC ship.

---

## ⚓ Remaining Port Time Activities (4:00 PM – 5:00 PM)

* **Eidfjord Old Church (Eidfjord Gamle Kyrkje)**
  Take a flat, 5-minute (400-meter) walk down the smooth sidewalk to the historic stone church built in 1277. The grounds are quiet, level, and offer a peaceful look at local medieval history.
* **Waterfront Promenade Stroll & Souvenirs**
  Walk back along the completely flat, paved shoreline path. The older women can sit on the waterfront benches to photograph the cruise ship in the fjord, or browse the small, step-free gift shops right at the pier terminal building.
* **Final Tender Boarding**
  Walk the final few meters to the pier gates to board the tender boat back to the ship prior to the 5:00 PM cutoff.
